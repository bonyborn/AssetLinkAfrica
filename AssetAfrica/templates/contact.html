Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my asset?

Login to your dashboard, click "Add New Listing", and follow the step-by-step guide.

How long does verification take?

Standard verification takes 24-48 hours. Premium users get priority within 12 hours.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and never store your full payment details.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, cancellations are subject to the asset owner's policy. Contact support for assistance.

What countries do you serve?

We currently operate in Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, Uganda, and Tanzania.

How do I become a verified user?

Complete identity verification in your dashboard by submitting required documents.
