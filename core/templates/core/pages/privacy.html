Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Introduction

AssetLinkAfrica ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, website, and services.

Our Commitment: We are dedicated to being transparent about our data practices and giving you control over your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, bio, preferences, location
  • Listing Information: Property/vehicle details, photos, pricing, documents
  • Transaction Information: Booking history, payment status, and M-Pesa receipt references. We do not collect or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Communication: Messages, inquiries, support tickets

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, search queries, clicks
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address
  • Cookies: Tracking technologies to enhance user experience

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Social media profiles (if you connect your account)
  • Safaricom M-Pesa (payment processing and transaction status)
  • Verification services (ID verification)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

Process transactions Contract performance Payment, booking data
Communicate with users Legitimate interest Contact information
Improve and personalize experience Legitimate interest Usage data, preferences

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: When you list assets or make inquiries, certain information is visible to other users
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with payment processing, hosting, analytics, and customer support
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share your information
Important: When you post listings publicly, your information may be visible to all platform users. Please be mindful of what you share.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Access Controls: Strict authentication and authorization protocols
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Data Minimization: We only collect necessary information
  • Employee Training: Regular security awareness training for staff
Security Tip: Use a strong, unique password for your AssetLinkAfrica account and enable two-factor authentication when available.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax records for 7 years)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Legitimate business purposes

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. International Data Transfers

AssetLinkAfrica operates primarily in Africa. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries where we have operations or service providers.

We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses approved by relevant authorities.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

11. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.
